Government Schemes

Grow your farm with central and state government subsidies.

National Livestock Mission (NLM)

Avail up to 50% capital subsidy (up to ₹50 Lakhs) for rural poultry, sheep, goat, and piggery development.

Rashtriya Gokul Mission (RGM)

₹3,400 Crore scheme focusing on development and conservation of indigenous bovine breeds.

NABARD DEDS

Dairy Entrepreneurship Development Scheme offering 25–33% back-ended capital subsidy for bankable dairy projects.

National Programme for Dairy Development

₹2,790 Crore outlay to create and strengthen dairy infrastructure at the grassroots level.

Animal Husbandry ID Fund (AHIDF)

₹15,000 Crore fund offering 3% interest subvention for infrastructure investments.

PM Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Working capital loans for livestock farmers with interest subvention resulting in an effective rate of just 4%.

PM Livestock Insurance

Protection against animal death with 50–70% premium subsidy provided by the government.

Poultry Venture Capital

Financial assistance and back-ended subsidies up to ₹25 Lakh for encouraging poultry farming.

State-Level Schemes

Additional localized benefits in states like Telangana (Sheep distribution), AP, Maharashtra, and Rajasthan.

How to Apply: Register on the official NLM portal (nlm.dahd.gov.in) or visit your nearest NABARD nodal commercial bank branch for processing.
